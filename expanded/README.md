## My Learning

The dashboard derives a read-only summary from existing Adaptive Study history; it needs no new save schema, server or migration. Hard80 results are intentionally separate to preserve the exam boundary. Weeks summarize coverage; content areas expand to the existing topic focuses. Skills show overlapping tagged-question performance, not a diagnosis of why a response was wrong. Practice buttons populate the existing session setup and retain its replacement confirmation.

Evidence families and related variants inside a focus are grouped so repetition cannot inflate breadth or first-attempt accuracy. Not assessed means no answers. Fewer than 3 observed case groups stays limited evidence, including focuses whose inventory cannot support more. Needs practice requires at least 3 observed groups and 2 misses among the latest 5 distinct groups. Consistent on review requires at least 3 groups, correct/confident latest responses among up to 5 groups, and successful reviews of 2 groups in different sessions at least24 hours after their initial attempt. These are transparent, unvalidated study heuristics, not calibrated mastery or readiness. Confidence alone cannot improve a score. Repeats can indicate retention on seen cases, not transfer to new situations. Existing difficulty and grading algorithms are unchanged.
