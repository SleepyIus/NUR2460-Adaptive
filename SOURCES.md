# Content provenance and clinical boundaries

This repository contains original educational scenarios and rationales, not copied ATI questions, NCLEX items, instructor examination questions, or textbook chapters. All patient cases are fictional. Source documents were supplied for private study and are not redistributed here.

The bank is not independently reviewed or validated by a nursing educator. The version 3 author check is dated September 10, 2026; it is not clinical certification. Report a disputed answer with its item ID and revision, the scenario, and a supporting source. In actual care, follow current guidance, patient-specific orders, scope of practice, and institutional protocols.

## Selected clinical clarifications

Course notes include some statements that should not be carried into patient-care questions unchanged. Question-specific links identify supplemental guidance. Examples:

- Neonatal ventilation takes priority for apnea or persistent heart rate below 100/min after initial steps. Meconium alone does not require routine tracheal suction before ventilation. [AHA/AAP neonatal resuscitation, 2025](https://cpr.heart.org/en/resuscitation-science/cpr-and-ecc-guidelines/neonatal-resuscitation).
- Emergency contraception is not limited universally to 72 hours. Ulipristal has different instructions for restarting hormonal contraception than levonorgestrel. [CDC emergency contraception](https://www.cdc.gov/contraception/hcp/usspr/emergency-contraception.html).
- Preeclampsia can involve qualifying organ dysfunction without proteinuria, and significant postpartum symptoms still need urgent evaluation. [ACOG preeclampsia guidance](https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/preeclampsia-and-high-blood-pressure-during-pregnancy).
- CMV prevention includes avoiding exposure to young children's urine and saliva; a droplet-only explanation is insufficient. [CDC CMV information](https://www.cdc.gov/cytomegalovirus/about/index.html).
- Reflux usually does not justify inclined, side, or prone infant sleep. [AAP safe sleep with reflux](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/tips-tools/ask-the-pediatrician/Pages/What-is-the-safest-sleep-solution-for-my-baby-with-reflux.aspx).
- A fresh gastrostomy tract needs specialist guidance after displacement. [Royal Children's Hospital gastrostomy guidance](https://www.rch.org.au/clinicalguide/guideline_index/Gastrostomy_Common_problems/).

Medication regimens, immunization recommendations, procedural thresholds, and local policies can change. The bank avoids treating a dated teaching shortcut as a universal clinical order. Ask your instructor about differences that may affect the course's intended examination answer.

## Authorship history

Version 2 contains 240 items: 80 original questions from the earlier locally prepared practice PDF, 21 original pregnancy-pilot additions, and 139 original expansion scenarios. The retained 80 were not copied from a commercial question bank. Related conditions recur through different care decisions; this is targeted practice, not 240 unrelated diagnoses.

Version 3 contains those 240 revised items plus 12 new cases (252 current questions). Each received an explicit reasoning-based difficulty judgment. The pass also separated duplicated clues from explanations, added source locators, and corrected q47 to specify levonorgestrel emergency contraception rather than mixing product-dependent restart instructions. The 240 archived version 2 items are retained only for historical answers and unfinished older exams; they are not 240 additional current-bank questions. The original ambiguous q47 is visibly flagged when an archived rationale is reviewed.

Some prior items are reused deliberately so study progress can distinguish fresh from repeated encounters. The bank is finite. Three times the topic counts does not guarantee three entirely nonoverlapping exams: format, skill, and difficulty requirements can require repeated items across sessions.
